# AI Summit India 2025

## Global IndiaAI Summit 2025

### Overview
- **Date:** February 10–11, 2025
- **Location:** Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi
- **Organizer:** Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY), IndiaAI Mission
- **Theme:** "Advancing AI for Global Good"
- **Scale:** 80+ countries, 1,500+ delegates, 100+ speakers

### Key Announcements

#### IndiaAI Mission Initiatives
- **IndiaAI Compute:** 10,000+ GPU cluster being deployed for public AI research
- **IndiaAI Innovation Centre:** Hub for developing foundational AI models
- **IndiaAI Datasets Platform:** Curated Indian-language and domain-specific datasets
- **IndiaAI FutureSkills:** Training 500,000+ Indians in AI by 2027
- **IndiaAI Startup Financing:** ₹2,000 crore fund for AI startups

#### Policy & Governance
- India positioned as leader in "AI for development" narrative
- Emphasis on responsible AI, sovereignty of data
- Multilingual AI models for 22 official Indian languages
- AI safety framework discussions with global partners

#### International Collaborations
- US-India AI partnership deepening
- EU-India dialogue on AI regulation
- ASEAN AI cooperation framework
- Africa-India AI capacity building

### Key Speakers & Sessions
- Government leaders from 80+ nations
- Tech CEOs from Google, Microsoft, Meta, NVIDIA
- Academic researchers from IITs, IISc, MIT, Stanford
- Startup founders from Indian AI ecosystem

### India's AI Strategy Pillars
1. **Compute Infrastructure** — Build sovereign AI compute capacity
2. **Data** — Create high-quality Indian datasets
3. **Foundational Models** — Develop India-specific LLMs
4. **Talent** — Train next-gen AI workforce
5. **Startups** — Fund and accelerate AI startups
6. **Ethics** — Responsible AI governance framework
