## Seminal Papers (Background Reading)

1. **Shor (1994)** — Polynomial-time algorithms for prime factorization and discrete logarithms on a quantum computer
2. **Grover (1996)** — A fast quantum mechanical algorithm for database search
3. **Preskill (2018)** — Quantum Computing in the NISQ era and beyond
4. **Arute et al. (2019)** — Quantum supremacy using a programmable superconducting processor (Google Sycamore)
5. **Kitaev (2003)** — Fault-tolerant quantum computation by anyons (topological quantum computing)
