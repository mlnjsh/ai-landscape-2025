## Hot Investment Categories

### 1. Foundation Models & LLMs
- Massive rounds for frontier labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral)
- Open-source model companies gaining traction
- Vertical-specific LLMs (legal, medical, financial)

### 2. AI Infrastructure
- GPU cloud providers (CoreWeave, Lambda, Together AI)
- Custom AI chips (Cerebras, Groq, SambaNova)
- MLOps and AI deployment platforms
- Vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Chroma)

### 3. AI Agents & Automation
- Autonomous coding agents
- Customer service AI agents
- RPA + AI workflow automation
- Multi-agent orchestration frameworks

### 4. AI Applications
- AI-native search (Perplexity)
- AI video/image generation (Runway, Stability, Pika)
- AI coding assistants (Cursor, Replit, Codeium)
- AI-powered design tools (Figma AI, Canva AI)

### 5. AI Safety & Alignment
- Growing investment in safety research
- Interpretability tools and frameworks
- AI evaluation and red-teaming companies
