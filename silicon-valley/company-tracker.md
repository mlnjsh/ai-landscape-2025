### Meta AI (FAIR)

### xAI

- **CEO:** Elon Musk

### Mistral AI
- **Valuation:** ~$6B (Dec 2024)
- **Key Products:** Mistral Large, Mixtral, Pixtral, Codestral

### Amazon (AWS + Alexa AI)
- **Key Products:** Bedrock, Nova models, Trainium chips, Alexa LLM
- **Latest:** Custom Trainium2 AI chips; Nova foundation models; $4B+ investment in Anthropic; Bedrock expanding model selection

## AI Regulation & Policy

### Key Developments
- **EU AI Act** — Enforcement begins 2025, risk-based classification of AI systems
- **US Executive Order on AI** — Safety testing requirements for powerful models
- **California SB 1047** — Vetoed but sparked national debate on AI safety legislation
- **China AI regulations** — Deepfake laws, generative AI service rules enacted
- **India** — No regulation yet; focus on innovation-first approach; IndiaAI Mission

### Industry Self-Regulation
- Frontier Model Forum (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft)
- Voluntary safety commitments at AI Safety Summit (Bletchley Park, Seoul)
- Model evaluation frameworks (METR, Apollo Research, ARC)
