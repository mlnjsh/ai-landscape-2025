# AI Policy & Regulation Tracker

## Global AI Regulation Overview

### European Union — AI Act
- **Status:** Enforcement phased from Feb 2025
- **Approach:** Risk-based classification (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal risk)
- **Key provisions:**
  - Banned: Social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance (with exceptions)
  - High-risk: Healthcare AI, hiring algorithms, law enforcement tools
  - Transparency: Must disclose AI-generated content, chatbot interactions
  - Foundation models: Compliance obligations for "general-purpose AI" providers
- **Fines:** Up to 7% of global annual turnover

### United States
- **Executive Order on AI (Oct 2023):** Safety testing for powerful models, NIST AI safety standards
- **CHIPS Act:** $280B for semiconductor manufacturing (indirect AI impact)
- **State-level:** California's SB 1047 vetoed but set precedent for state AI bills
- **Congress:** Multiple AI bills proposed, no comprehensive federal law yet
- **2025 outlook:** New administration may adjust approach to AI regulation

### China
- **Regulations enacted:**
  - Deepfake regulations (Jan 2023)
  - Generative AI service rules (Aug 2023)
  - Algorithm recommendation regulations
  - AI ethics guidelines
- **Approach:** Control-oriented, require licensing for public AI services
- **Strategic goals:** AI self-sufficiency, domestic chip development

### India
- **Current stance:** Innovation-first, no comprehensive AI regulation
- **IndiaAI Mission:** Focus on development, not restriction
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023):** Covers data used for AI training
- **Future:** Advisory approach preferred over hard regulation

### United Kingdom
- **AI Safety Institute** established (2023)
- **Bletchley Declaration** on AI safety (Nov 2023)
- **Seoul AI Safety Summit** co-hosted (May 2024)
- **Approach:** Pro-innovation, sector-specific regulation

### Open Questions in AI Governance
1. **Liability:** Who is responsible when AI causes harm?
2. **Copyright:** Can AI models train on copyrighted data?
3. **Employment:** How to handle AI-driven job displacement?
4. **Deepfakes:** How to regulate synthetic media in elections?
5. **Concentration:** Should AI development be more distributed?
6. **Military AI:** Autonomous weapons governance
7. **Open-source:** Should powerful models be freely available?

### Emerging Framework
- **Fair use debate:** No clear legal precedent for AI training yet
- **Opt-out mechanisms:** robots.txt, AI training exclusion headers
- **Licensing deals:** OpenAI-AP, OpenAI-Axel Springer, Google-Reddit
- **EU approach:** Right to opt out of AI training for European content
